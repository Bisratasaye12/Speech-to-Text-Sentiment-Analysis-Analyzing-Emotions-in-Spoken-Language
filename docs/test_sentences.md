# Test Sentences for Emotion Classification

Use these sentences to test your text emotion model. Speak them clearly into the microphone.

## 😠 Anger
- "I'm so frustrated with this situation right now!"
- "This is absolutely ridiculous and unacceptable!"
- "I can't believe you did that, I'm really angry!"
- "This makes me furious!"

## 🤢 Disgust
- "That's absolutely disgusting, I can't stand it!"
- "Ew, that's so gross and revolting!"
- "I find that completely repulsive!"
- "That's sickening, I hate it!"

## 😨 Fear
- "I'm really scared about what might happen next."
- "This situation is terrifying and I'm worried."
- "I'm afraid something bad is going to happen."
- "I feel nervous and anxious about this."

## 😊 Happy
- "I'm so excited and happy about this news!"
- "This is amazing, I'm thrilled!"
- "I feel great and really joyful today!"
- "I'm delighted and grateful for this opportunity!"

## 😐 Neutral
- "The weather today is partly cloudy."
- "I need to go to the store later."
- "The meeting is scheduled for three o'clock."
- "I have three items on my shopping list."

## 😢 Sad
- "I'm feeling really down and disappointed."
- "This is so sad and heartbreaking."
- "I feel miserable and hopeless right now."
- "I'm really upset and feeling blue today."

---

## Tips for Testing:
1. **Speak naturally** - Don't over-act, just speak as you normally would
2. **Vary your tone** - Try different intonations for the same sentence
3. **Test edge cases** - Try sentences that might be ambiguous
4. **Compare results** - See if similar emotions get similar predictions

## Expected Behavior:
- Sentences with strong emotion words should get high confidence
- Neutral sentences should predict "neutral" with reasonable confidence
- The model may struggle with subtle emotions or sarcasm (this is normal)
